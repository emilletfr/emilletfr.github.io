<h1>iDictation</h1>

<p class="lede">Make spelling a regular appointment. With iDictation, listen, write and understand your mistakes. The daily dictation is free every day, and the first dictation of each level is free forever.</p>

<h3>THE DAILY DICTATION: A GOOD HABIT TO KEEP</h3>
<p>A text selected every day to make you want to come back and write. From the main menu, tap "Do today's dictation", listen to the sections at your own pace and move on to the correction. An Internet connection is needed to download it.</p>

<h3>A REMINDER SO YOU DON'T MISS IT</h3>
<p>Turn on the reminder from the menu: your device tells you when the daily dictation is waiting. Snooze it with a tap if it isn't the right moment, or turn it off whenever you like. The reminder lives on your device, with no sign-up.</p>

<h3>LISTEN, WRITE, PROGRESS</h3>
<p>Rediscover the pleasure of dictation on iPhone and iPad. Listen to the text, type what you hear directly in the app, then read your work back. Practice spelling, grammar, agreements and conjugation through varied texts and literary extracts.</p>

<h3>TAKE YOUR TIME TO WRITE</h3>
<p>Missed a passage? Replay it as often as you need, or go back to the previous one. The slow sections give you time to write. You decide when to move on — no one needs to dictate the text for you.</p>

<h3>SPOT YOUR MISTAKES, UNDERSTAND THE RULES</h3>
<p>The visual correction compares your work with the expected text. Tap a mistake: you get an explanation and a tip to understand it better. Each dictation includes one explanation; unlocking the catalog gives three per dictation, and the iDictation Unlimited subscription makes them unlimited. These explanations use an Internet connection and their availability may be limited.</p>

<h3>REVIEW YOUR MISTAKES, ONE BY ONE</h3>
<p>The revision notebook is included with the full catalog purchase or the iDictation Unlimited subscription: it gathers your mistakes on your device, with their explanation, and turns them into exercises. In a session, the original sentence is dictated: listen to it, retype the right word, and the fixed word leaves the notebook.</p>

<h3>TRACK YOUR PROGRESS</h3>
<p>Nothing is more motivating than seeing how far you have come. "My progress" brings together your real figures: your day streak, your corrected dictations, your average score, your latest scores and the state of your notebook. Your league, from Beginner to Expert, moves up with every corrected dictation. With the full catalog purchase or the iDictation Unlimited subscription, share a progress report: ideal for following a child's progress, or simply staying on track.</p>

<h3>A CATALOGUE TO VARY YOUR PRACTICE</h3>
<p>Choose from 3 difficulty levels, check your results and redo the catalog dictations to try to improve your score. Training for your studies, a refresher, or simply the pleasure of writing it right.</p>

<h3>WHO IS IT FOR?</h3>
<p>For students — from 8 to 14 years old — and for adults who want to brush up on their spelling.</p>

<h3>GETTING STARTED</h3>
<p>• A new dictation, free every day. • The first dictation of each level, free forever. • One explanation included per dictation. • A daily reminder, if you want one. • Unlock the full catalog with a single in-app purchase. • The revision notebook is included with the catalog purchase or iDictation Unlimited: your mistakes gathered, to retype one by one. • Track your progress: day streak, scores, notebook, league — the report can be shared with the purchase or subscription. • Move up to iDictation Unlimited: the whole catalog and unlimited explanations. • Already bought the full version? Your unlimited explanations stay yours.</p>

<p>Your next appointment with words starts here.</p>
